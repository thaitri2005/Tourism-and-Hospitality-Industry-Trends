## Corrections made

1. Removed standalone `Secretary-General` and `chairman/chairperson` as triggers for “Partnerships, governance and institutions.” Those office titles alone caused 671 historical assignments, including 48 of 551 current-window articles, without establishing that the story concerned governance or partnership. Explicit terms such as `partnership`, `governance`, `general assembly`, and `board of directors` remain.
2. Added `biometrics`/`biometric` to “AI and digital technology” after the live WTTC example showed the omission.
3. Rebuilt the analysis offline with codebook version 0.4. In the current window, the partnership/governance candidate fell from 219 to 171 articles (39.8% to 31.0%); it remains a candidate mention rate, not a validated topic prevalence.
4. Updated the report to say NMF is only a diagnostic: large clusters are publisher-, institution-, brand-, or format-driven. The model should not be presented as independent confirmation of substantive themes.

The generated review template is now an optional legacy artifact. The subsequent automated workflow reviews every saved record and assignment, applies curation corrections and records focus/context and sentiment-model sensitivity. No human annotation step is required to complete that workflow. Neither this earlier spot-check nor the automated audit supplies labelled sentiment accuracy.
